Apple Cinnamon Streusel Muffins

BreakfastIndex
PreviousNext

Ingredients

1 1/2 cups flour
1/2 cup sugar
2 teaspoons baking powder
1/2 teaspoon salt
1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
1 egg
1/2 cup milk
1/4 cup vegetable oil
1 cup grated apple

Streusel Topping

1/4 cup flour
2 tablespoons brown sugar
1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
2 tablespoons butter

Streusel Directions

1

Combine flour, brown sugar, and cinnamon in a small mixing bowl.

2

Cut in butter with a pastry blender until well mixed and crumbly.

3

Sprinkle over muffins before baking.

Directions

1

Preheat oven to 400 degrees.

2

In a large mixing bowl, combine the dry ingredients. Make a well in the center and set aside.

3

In a medium mixing bowl, beat the egg, milk, and oil. Stir in apple.

4

Add liquid mixture to the well of the dry ingredients, stirring just until moistened. Batter will be slightly lumpy.

5

Spoon into greased muffin pans or line with muffin papers.

6

Top each muffin with prepared streusel topping.

7

Bake for 20 to 25 minutes.

Applesauce Cinnamon Muffins

BreakfastIndex
PreviousNext

Ingredients

2 cups flour
1 teaspoon baking soda
1 teaspoon allspice
2 teaspoons cinnamon
1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
1/2 cup margarine
1 cup sugar
1 egg
1/2 teaspoon vanilla
1 cup applesauce

Directions

1

Preheat oven to 375 degrees.

2

In a large mixing bowl, combine flour, baking soda, allspice, cinnamon, and cloves.

3

Make a well and set aside.

4

In an electric mixer bowl, cream together margarine and sugar.

5

Add egg, vanilla, and applesauce and mix until well combined.

6

Add butter mixture to the well of the dry ingredients and mix until blended.

7

Spoon into greased muffin cups, or line with muffin liners, filling about 2/3 full.

8

Bake for 20 minutes.

Biscuits Supreme with Sausage Gravy

BreakfastIndex
PreviousNext

Biscuit Ingredients

2 cups flour
3 teaspoons baking powder
2 teaspoons sugar
1 1/2 teaspoons cream of tartar
1/4 teaspoon salt
1/2 cup shortening
2/3 cup milk

Sausage Gravy Ingredients

1/2 pound sausage
3 tablespoons flour
1/2 teaspoon salt, plus pepper
1 1/2 cups milk

Biscuit Directions

1

Preheat oven to 450 degrees F.

2

Stir together flour, baking powder, sugar, cream of tartar, and salt.

3

Cut in shortening until mixture resembles coarse crumbs.

4

Make a well in the center and add milk all at once. Stir just until dough clings together.

5

Knead gently on a lightly floured surface for 10 to 12 strokes.

6

Pat into 1-inch thickness. Cut with a biscuit cutter, dipping in flour between cuts.

7

Place on an ungreased cookie sheet.

8

Bake at 450 degrees F for 10 to 12 minutes.

Gravy Directions

1

Brown sausage in a skillet.

2

Remove from heat and add flour, salt, and pepper.

3

Add milk and return to heat until thick and bubbly.

4

Serve with biscuits.

Notes

Use baking powder.
Use cream of tartar.
Use pastry cloth.
Pat dough 1 inch thick; do not make into a cracker.
Be careful to use the correct amount of milk.

Cranberry Orange Muffins

BreakfastIndex
PreviousNext

Ingredients

2 cups all-purpose flour
2 teaspoons baking powder
1/2 teaspoon salt
1 cup granulated sugar
Zest of 1 orange
1/2 cup vegetable oil
1/2 cup milk
2 large eggs
1/4 cup orange juice
2 teaspoons vanilla extract
1 1/2 cups fresh or frozen cranberries

Directions

1

Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F. Line a 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners.

2

In a large bowl, sift together flour, baking powder, and salt.

3

In another bowl, combine sugar and orange zest. Work the zest into the sugar with your fingertips until evenly combined and the sugar feels like wet sand.

4

Whisk in vegetable oil, milk, eggs, orange juice, and vanilla until well combined.

5

Pour the sugar mixture into the flour mixture and stir just until no streaks of flour remain.

6

Fold in the cranberries. Divide batter among the paper liners. Sprinkle with more sugar, if desired.

7

Bake for 25 minutes, or until the tops are golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Frozen cranberries may need a few extra minutes.

8
